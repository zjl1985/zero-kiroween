# Zero and Nicole model sheet

[Official heads](official-heads.png) were rasterized from `officialRects` in `src/cats/official.ts`, using the Meow Wall palettes and pixels in `src/cats/meow-wall-art.ts`. Zero is on the left and Nicole on the right.

The built-in ImageGen tool generated the [model sheet](raw/zero-nicole-model-sheet.png) with those heads as its reference. The two rows correspond to Zero and Nicole. Each row shows the front, front three-quarter and back three-quarter poses. `scripts/iso-art/reference_cats.py` cuts the two three-quarter poses into the [original-resolution game atlas](../render/cat-reference-atlas.png). The visible RGB values are checked against the source. The game draws each crop at display resolution and mirrors the supplied views for the opposite facings.

## Generation request

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create one pixel-art character design reference sheet for our original HD-2D browser game Midnight Meow. The attached image shows the EXACT official character head artwork: Zero on the LEFT and Nicole on the RIGHT. Use it as an identity and geometry reference, never the earlier voxel avatars. Make full bodies for these exact heads. Keep the visibly wide, squat silhouette, small squared tall ears in the same positions, thick dark stair-step edges, protruding straight pixel whiskers, deep rectangular spectacles at the identical spacing and height, very small black nose and inverted Y mouth, exact muzzle/light fur shapes, and hair markings. Keep faces forward facing and preserve the official pixels on the forward heads as closely as possible. Zero's low gold forelock and two black sunglass lenses with white square highlights, warm tan fur, black coat and tiny yellow chain; Nicole's dark asymmetric hair framing her cheeks, pink cheek marks, rectangular black glasses with square glints, pale white muzzle, white T-shirt and deep blue cuffed jeans. Give each a very compact, feline chibi body: front head width (without whiskers) about twice shoulder width, head is roughly 55% of the standing height, short triangular arms with visible cat paws and two short separated feet, short curled tail low at the hip. Zero's dark jacket drapes behind his paws, Nicole's simple white tee has a little blue shadow. They should be delightful and poised, each with a slightly different natural stance. One wide clean studio sheet, strictly two rows and three columns. Row 1 all Zero, row 2 all Nicole. Column 1 direct front, column 2 isometric front three-quarter looking screen-right and slightly down, column 3 isometric rear three-quarter looking screen-right and up. Entire body and feet in frame with generous separation. Large clear readable pixels; use one coherent, crisply stair-stepped 48–56 px logical palette-sprite style per pose, 3–4 shades per material, no gradients, no antialiasing, no 3D toy-like rendering or thin detail. Flat neutral pale gray backing so shapes can be traced. No world, stand, icons, lettering, watermarks or shadows beyond faint contact under the feet. This is a model sheet for redrawing game sprites, so front-facing head fidelity and clear body silhouettes are the priority.
